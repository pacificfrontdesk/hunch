Privacy Policy

Hunch · Nattskift Games · last updated 9 September 2026

Who we are

Hunch is published by Pacific Front Desk LLC, doing business as Nattskift Games ("Nattskift Games", "we", "us"). Pacific Front Desk LLC is the entity responsible for the app and for this policy.

The short version

Hunch does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Everything the game knows about you stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following locally, and nowhere else:

  • Which puzzles you have played and how many guesses each took
  • Your current and longest streak
  • Your settings — chosen board, colour-blind mode, sound, haptics, reminders
  • Whether you have purchased the full game

Deleting the app deletes all of it. We never see any of it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Hunch submits two numbers to Apple's leaderboards: your longest streak and how many puzzles you have solved. Your Game Center nickname is shown beside them to other players. This is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy — we never receive your Apple Account details.

You can play the entire game without signing in. Only the leaderboards need it.

Purchases

The full game is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. Payment details are never sent to us and we never see them. We receive only a signal from Apple that the purchase is valid.

Notifications

If you allow it, Hunch sends one reminder a day when a new puzzle is available. The notification is scheduled on your device and nothing is sent from a server. You can turn it off in the app, or in the Settings app.

Children

Hunch is rated 4+ and is safe for children. Since it collects nothing, there is nothing to collect from a child either.

Changes

If this ever changes — if a future version collects anything at all — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than buried.
