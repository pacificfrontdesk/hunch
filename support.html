Support

Hunch · Nattskift Games

Get in touch

Email hunch@pacificfrontdesk.com. One person reads it, so a reply usually takes a day or two rather than an hour.

It helps to include your device, your iOS version, and the puzzle number shown as DAY next to HUNCH at the top of the board.

Common questions

I lost my streak

Streaks are kept on your device. Deleting the app, resetting the device, or moving to a new phone without a backup will clear it, and we cannot restore it — there is no account and no server holding a copy. Signing in to Game Center keeps your longest streak on the leaderboard even when the local one resets.

I paid but the archive is locked

Open the store from the bag icon at the top of the board and tap Restore purchase. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account, so make sure you are signed in with the same one you bought it with.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

The leaderboard is empty or missing

Leaderboards need Game Center. Open the Settings app, tap Game Center, and check you are signed in. Scores can take a minute to appear. The puzzle itself works fully offline and never needs a connection.

I cannot tell two colours apart

Open Settings in the app and turn on Patterns as well as colour. Every colour then gets its own fill, so the board can be read without relying on hue.

Can I play yesterday's puzzle?

Yes, with the full game — it unlocks every puzzle back to the first. Archive puzzles are recorded separately and never affect your streak.
